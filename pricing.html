      Extra dogs

      The second dog adds £2.50 a visit, the third £2, and any after that £1.50 each. It tapers because a second dog in the same garden isn’t twice the work — it’s the walking about that takes the time, not the picking up.

      First visit

      If the garden has a backlog, the first visit carries a one-off catch-up charge on top — between £6 and £40 depending on how long it’s been and how big the space is. Every visit after that is the standard rate. The builder shows you both numbers separately.
