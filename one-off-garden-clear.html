Gardens get away from people. Constantly.

Illness, a new baby, a bad stretch, a rescue dog you inherited a situation with, or just a long wet winter where nobody went down the end of the garden. It happens to reasonable people all the time.

You will not get a comment, a raised eyebrow or a joke about it. We have genuinely seen worse, and the entire job is to make it stop being a thing you think about.

  • Whole area cleared — lawn, borders, patio, paths, gravel, behind the shed
  • Bugalugs sanitising treatment included as standard
  • Photographed when done, so you have proof for a landlord or agent
  • No obligation to book anything afterwards
