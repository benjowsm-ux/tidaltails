How it works

The bit nobody wants is the storing

Most people don’t actually mind picking it up. What they mind is the bag sitting in the wheelie bin for nine days in August, and the bin smelling of it long after collection day.

So we hand you a 23-litre lockable caddy that lives outside. You put the bags straight in, the lid keeps it sealed, and on your area’s day we empty it and take the contents away with us.

  • From £7.50 a collection — cheaper than us doing the scooping
  • Sealed lockable caddy, so nothing smells and nothing gets into it
  • Same day every week, decided by your part of Weston
  • Waste taken off your property entirely, not left in your bin
  • Caddy supplied for a one-off £20, or use your own and pay nothing
  • Add a rinse-and-deodorise on every swap for £3
