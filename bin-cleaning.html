What we do

Rinsed, treated, and not just splashed out

We empty it, rinse it through, and treat it with the same pet-safe Bugalugs products we use on the garden. The residue that builds up on the base and under the lip is where the smell comes from, and that is the bit that gets attention.

It takes us a couple of minutes on a visit we are already making, which is why it is £3 rather than the £15 a dedicated bin-cleaning round would have to charge.
