Why people call us

There are only three reasons anyone books this

All three are completely valid, and we're not going to pretend otherwise.

You haven’t got the time

Between work, kids and everything else, the garden is the thing that slips. It’s twenty minutes a week you’d rather spend literally anywhere else.

It’s genuinely hard for you

Bad back, bad knees, a mobility aid, or you simply can’t bend like you used to. Your dog still deserves the walk — this bit shouldn’t be the barrier.

You just really hate doing it

Fair. It’s the worst job in the garden and there is no prize for doing it yourself. Hand it over and never think about it again.
