Between guests

The turnaround nobody schedules for

Changeover day is already tight. Beds, bins, bathrooms and a four-hour window — and the garden is the thing that gets a glance from the back door rather than a proper look.

We work to your changeover time, clear the whole outside space, sanitise it, and send you a photo before the next guests arrive. If it is clear, you get a photo saying so and pay nothing.

  • Scheduled around your changeover, not our convenience
  • Photographed every visit — proof for you and for the guest if it is ever disputed
  • End-of-tenancy clears for rentals, invoiced to you or the agent
  • Bugalugs sanitising as standard, which matters more with strangers’ dogs
  • No contract — use us for a season, or only in summer
  • One invoice a month across multiple properties
